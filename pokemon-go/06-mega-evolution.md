# 6. Mega Evolution

Mega Evolution is the highest-leverage thing a PvE player can do, because **it buffs
everyone in the lobby, not just you.** If you take one habit from this guide, make it
"Mega Evolve before every raid."

---

## 6.1 Why Megas matter so much

When you Mega Evolve in a raid:

| Effect | Value |
|---|---|
| Attacks matching the Mega's type get boosted, **for every trainer in the raid** | **+30%** |
| All other attacks get boosted, for every trainer | **+10%** |
| Bonus Candy on catching the raid boss | Yes |
| Bonus Candy XL chance | Yes |
| The Mega itself is usually the highest-DPS attacker available | Yes |

A single Mega Evolution can add more total damage to a lobby than an entire extra
trainer. **Mega Evolve even if the Mega isn't the ideal type** — the +10% is free.

**Best practice:** Mega Evolve a Pokémon that **shares a type with the counters
you're bringing**, so you get the +30% rather than the +10%.

---

## 6.2 Mega Energy and Mega Levels

Mega Energy is species-specific, earned from Mega Raids and some research.

**Mega Levels** track how often you've Mega Evolved a given species:

| Level | How to reach it |
|---|---|
| **Base** | First Mega Evolution |
| **High** | Mega Evolve repeatedly |
| **Max** | Keep Mega Evolving |
| **Super Max** ⭐ | Reach Max, then spend **5,000 Mega Energy** of that species |

Higher Mega Levels give:
- **Cheaper Mega Energy costs** to Mega Evolve again — and eventually **free**
  Mega Evolutions after a cooldown.
- **Shorter rest period** between free Mega Evolutions.
- Bigger catch-bonus multipliers.

---

## 6.3 Super Max Level (added February 2026)

A fourth Mega Level above Max. It is a significant PvE upgrade.

**Cost:** reach Max Level, then spend **5,000 Mega Energy** of that species.

**What you get:**

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Temporary +2 level boost** | While Mega Evolved — worth more than a full L40→L50 climb. |
| **A third Charged Attack** | Eligible species get an **extra Charged Attack while Mega Evolved**, and Mega Level adds a **bonus multiplier to its base power**. |
| **Rest period → 24 hours** | Free Mega Evolutions once a day. |
| **Greatly increased Catch Candy, Catch XP and Candy XL** | For Pokémon sharing a type with your Mega. This is a serious grinding tool. |

> **PvE verdict:** Super Max is worth chasing for **one or two Megas you use
> constantly** — typically your best generalist (Mega Rayquaza, Mega Mewtwo Y) and
> one Mega matching a type you farm heavily. 5,000 Mega Energy is a lot of Mega
> Raids; don't spread it.

---

## 6.4 Super Mega Raids

A new raid tier introduced alongside Super Max Level.

- Requires **at least 8 trainers, all Mega Evolved.**
- The boss has an **enrage/shield mechanic**: it deploys shields, and **each trainer
  can only break one shield.** This is why the headcount is a hard requirement — you
  literally cannot break enough shields with fewer people.
- Mega-Evolved attackers get **significantly powered-up attacks** when breaking
  shields.
- Rewards **substantially more Mega Energy** than standard Mega Raids — this is the
  fast route to 5,000 for Super Max.
- **Requires Link Charges** (a resource now needed for all Mega Raids). Remote
  Super Mega Raids need **both a Remote Raid Pass and Link Charges**.

---

## 6.5 Which Megas to prioritise

Prioritise by **how often the type is useful**, not by raw power:

| Priority | Mega | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mega Rayquaza** | Best Dragon and top Flying; Dragon bosses are constant. |
| 2 | **Mega Mewtwo Y** | Best Psychic and one of the highest raw DPS in the game. |
| 3 | **Mega Gengar** | Top Ghost — answers the many Psychic Legendaries. |
| 4 | **Mega Latios / Mega Garchomp** | Accessible Dragon/Ground coverage. |
| 5 | **Mega Blaziken / Mega Lucario** | Fighting — the widest coverage type. |
| 6 | **Mega Diancie / Mega Gardevoir** | Fairy and Rock coverage. |
| 7 | **Primal Groudon / Primal Kyogre** | Technically Primal Reversion, same system. Best Ground and Water in the game. |

> **Practical rule:** you only need to *own* the Mega-capable species once. A single
> good copy, Mega Evolved before every raid, does the job. Do not hoard Mega
> candidates.

---

## 6.6 Habits that pay off

1. **Mega Evolve before every single raid.** It costs nothing once the species is at
   a high Mega Level and rested.
2. **Mega Evolve before catch-heavy events** (Community Day, Spotlight Hour) with a
   Mega matching the featured Pokémon's type — the Candy and Candy XL bonus is
   substantial, especially at Super Max.
3. **Rotate through Mega Evolutions on cooldown** to grind Mega Levels toward free
   Mega Evolutions.
4. **Bank Mega Energy** rather than spending it on marginal species. 5,000 for
   Super Max on a Mega you actually use beats four species at base level.
