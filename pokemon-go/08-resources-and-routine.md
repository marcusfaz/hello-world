# 8. Resources & Daily Routine

A PvE player is limited by three things: **Stardust**, **Candy XL**, and **raid
passes**. Everything below is about maximising those with minimal effort.

---

## 8.1 The daily loop (5 minutes)

| Do this | Why |
|---|---|
| **Spin a Gym** | Free **daily Raid Pass**. Never waste one — a raid a day is your Legendary income. |
| **Collect the Daily Adventure Egg** | Level 15+. Hatches in **1 km** for **10,000 XP** plus items. Free progression toward the **level 80** cap. |
| **Catch your first Pokémon of the day** | Daily catch streak. **7th day in a row = big Stardust bonus.** |
| **Spin your first Stop of the day** | Daily spin streak. 7th day = bonus items and XP. |
| **Open Gifts** | Friendship progress → huge Stardust payouts at Great/Ultra/Best Friend. |
| **Check for a Rocket Balloon** | Free Shadow encounters and Mysterious Components. |
| **Feed your Buddy / walk it** | Progress toward **Best Buddy** (+1 free level on an attacker). |
| **Collect Max Particles** if you pass a Power Spot | 800/day cap, resets 5 a.m. |
| **Collect Gym coins** | Cap is **50 coins/day**. Keep a few bulky Pokémon in Gyms. |

---

## 8.2 Stardust: the real bottleneck

**Income, best to worst:**

1. **Star Piece (+50%) during a high-catch event.** Community Day, Spotlight Hour,
   Incense Day. This is 3–4× normal income for the same time spent.
2. **Friendship level-ups.** Best Friend gives a very large one-off payout. Add
   friends, send gifts daily, and stagger your friendships so payouts land often.
3. **Daily catch/spin streaks.** Free.
4. **Field Research with Stardust rewards.** Bank the tasks, complete them all under
   a Star Piece.
5. **Raids.** Reliable dust per raid.

**Spending:** see [Investment §3.6](03-investment.md#36-where-to-spend-stardust-in-priority-order).
Short version — **six attackers at Level 40 beat three at Level 50.**

## 8.3 Candy XL: the hard cap on your best Pokémon

You need **296** (normal) / **360** (Shadow) / **272** (Purified) to take one Pokémon
from Level 40 to 50. Sources:

| Source | Yield |
|---|---|
| **Catching** | 0–3 per catch; **evolved and Legendary forms drop more reliably**. |
| **Raid bosses** | Minimum 1 guaranteed. |
| **Hatching eggs** | 1–4, scaling with egg distance. |
| **Trading** | 1 guaranteed if the two Pokémon were caught **100 km+** apart. |
| **Transferring** | Random, roughly **1 in 15–20**. |
| **Converting** | **100 regular Candy → 1 Candy XL.** Brutal exchange rate; last resort. |
| **Walking your Buddy** | Chance scales with your Pokémon's level. |
| **Mega Evolution active** | Increased drop rate for matching-type catches — **greatly increased at Super Max Mega Level.** |
| **Rare Candy XL** | Converts to any species. Save these for Legendaries you can't farm. |

**Strategy:** Candy XL comes overwhelmingly from **catching lots of the species you
care about**. Community Days and raid days for a species you want at Level 50 are
worth far more than any grinding you can do afterwards.

> **Save Rare Candy XL for Legendaries.** You can farm Machamp XL by catching Machop;
> you cannot farm Mewtwo XL.

## 8.4 Raid passes

| Pass | Get it from |
|---|---|
| **Free daily Raid Pass** | Spinning any Gym. **One per day, doesn't stack.** |
| **Premium Battle Pass** | Shop, research, level-up rewards. |
| **Remote Raid Pass** | Shop (limited purchases per week) and events. Daily remote-raid limit applies. |

**Efficiency rule:** the free daily pass is your baseline. Use it on the **hardest
raid you can reliably win**, prioritising:
1. A Legendary you don't have.
2. **Mega Energy** for a Mega you're pushing toward Super Max.
3. **Candy XL** for a species you're taking to Level 50.

## 8.5 Item management

Bag space is finite. Ruthless defaults:

- **Keep:** Golden Razz (raid catching), Max Revives, Max Potions, Ultra Balls, Rare
  Candy, Star Pieces, Lucky Eggs, Incubators.
- **Delete freely:** Poké Balls when full on Ultra, regular Razz/Nanab/Pinap once
  you're over ~40, Potions/Revives (keep Max versions), excess Berries.
- **Silver Pinap** for anything you want both candy and a catch from.
- **Golden Razz on every raid boss throw** — always.

## 8.6 XP and the level 80 cap

The cap went from 50 to **80 in October 2025**, and the whole curve was rebalanced so
levels come more frequently. Higher trainer level raises the CP of your wild catches
and unlocks better items, so it's worth pushing.

**Biggest XP sources:**
- **Lucky Egg + mass evolution.** Save 30–60 evolvable Pokémon (search `evolve`),
  pop a Lucky Egg, evolve them all. Still the single fastest XP in the game.
- **Daily Adventure Egg** — 10,000 XP for a 1 km walk, every day, free.
- **Raids** — large flat XP, doubled with a Lucky Egg.
- **Friendship level-ups** — enormous XP alongside the Stardust.
- **Excellent Curveball throws** and **new Pokédex entries**.

Stack a **Lucky Egg** with a friendship level-up and a batch of evolutions for a huge
single-shot payout.

## 8.7 Other systems worth using

- **Routes** — guided walking paths. Earn a Route Badge, **faster Buddy Candy**, an
  XP bonus, and **Zygarde Cells**. Free value on a walk you're taking anyway.
- **Party Play** — up to 4 trainers. Bonus challenges and a bonus attack meter in
  raids. Worth forming with even one friend.
- **Adventure Effects** — certain Legendaries (Origin Forme Dialga/Palkia, the
  Necrozma fusions) grant field bonuses when set as your buddy. Check what yours does.
- **Heal All button** — mass-revive between raids instead of tapping individually.
- **Ready Up button** — synchronises raid lobbies.

## 8.8 Events worth clearing your calendar for

| Event | Why it matters for PvE |
|---|---|
| **Community Day** | Exclusive charged move on evolution, 3× Candy, high spawn rate. The cheapest way to build a full six-strong team of one species, plus Candy XL. |
| **Raid Day / Raid Hour** | Bulk Legendary catches and Candy XL. |
| **Frustration-removal events** | The **only** cheap way to fix your Shadow backlog. Highest priority of all. |
| **Max Mondays** | Extra Power Spots, featured Dynamax, and bonuses like a Rare Candy XL. |
| **Spotlight Hour** | Often 2× Candy or 2× XP or 2× Stardust. Stack with a Star Piece. |
| **GO Fest / GO Tour** | Legendary variety and Mega Energy in bulk. |
