# 9. Cheat Sheet

One page. Everything you need at the moment of decision.

---

## Keep or Transfer?

**Never transfer:** shiny · costume · new dex entry · Dynamax/Gigantamax · Legendary ·
Mythical · Lucky · a Shadow of a good attacker · Community Day move · in a Gym · your Buddy.

**Otherwise:**

| Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Is the species (or its evolution) a top-6 attacker of its type? | Continue ↓ | **Transfer**, unless it's trade fodder or candy you need |
| Do you already have **six** good copies? | Keep only if Attack IV beats your worst keeper | **Keep** |

**Attack IV bar:** 13+ = as good as perfect. 10–12 = fine filler. Below 10 = only if
hard to re-catch.

**Ignore the star rating** — it's PvP-weighted. Look at the **Attack** bar only.

---

## Invest or Not?

```
EVOLVE  →  fix the MOVESET  →  get to SIX copies  →  POWER UP to 40  →  BEST BUDDY  →  (rarely) Level 50
```

| Decision | PvE answer |
|---|---|
| Evolve? | Yes, if final form of a useful line, a new dex entry, or an event move is live. |
| Power up? | To **Level 40**, if it's a top attacker with the right moveset and you have six. |
| Level 50? | Only your **top 3–6 Pokémon in the game**. +6.3% stats for 296 Candy XL + 250k dust. |
| Best Buddy? | **Always**, for anything you raid with. Free +1 level. |
| **Second charged move?** | **No** — it's a PvP expense. Exception: 2–3 Rocket-battle specialists. |
| Elite TM? | Only for legacy / Community Day moves. Never for a move in the normal pool. |
| Purify a Shadow? | **No**, if it's a good attacker. The 1.2× multiplier ≫ +2 IVs. |

## Build-order for a new-ish PvE roster

1. Six **Machamp** (Fighting — widest coverage).
2. Six **Mamoswine** (Ice + Ground — answers Dragons, the most common Legendary type).
3. Six **Rampardos** or **Rhyperior** (Rock).
4. Six **Chandelure** (Ghost + Fire).
5. Six **Metagross** (Steel).
6. Six **Garchomp** (Ground + Dragon).
7. Any Mega you can get — Mega Evolve before **every** raid.
8. Fill the remaining types opportunistically from raids and Community Days.

All at **Level 40**. Stop there until the roster is complete.

## Useful storage searches

**Transfer fodder — shows only Pokémon not worth keeping.** Tag your PvE roster
`keep` first, then paste:

```
!4*&!3*&!shiny&!legendary&!mythical&!ultra beast&!shadow&!purified&!lucky&!costume&!dynamax&!gigantamax&!favorite&!defender&!buddy&!evolvenew&!tradeevolve&!keep
```

Short version: `!4*&!3*&!shiny&!legendary&!mythical&!shadow&!lucky&!favorite&!evolvenew&!keep`

**Eyeball the list before confirming. Transfers are permanent.**

> **Never mix `&` and `,` in a cull search.** Sources disagree on whether `a,b&c`
> means `(a OR b) AND c` or `a OR (b AND c)`, and there are no parentheses to
> disambiguate. Under the wrong parse a trailing `1*,2*` surfaces *every* 2★ you own,
> shinies and legendaries included. Use `!4*&!3*` instead — same result, unambiguous.

**Rule-of-Six overflow**, one species at a time (commas alone are safe):

```
machop,machoke,machamp
```
Sort by CP, keep six, transfer the tail.

| Search | Finds |
|---|---|
| `age0` | Caught today |
| `evolvenew` | Evolving gives a new dex entry |
| `evolve` | Can evolve now |
| `distance5000-` | Candy XL trade fodder |
| `dynamax,gigantamax` | Max Battle roster |
| `shadow` | Your Frustration backlog |
| `@2frustration` | Shadows still stuck with Frustration |
| `4*` | Best IVs |

**Star bands (total IVs / 45):** 0★ 0–22 · 1★ 23–29 · 2★ 30–36 · 3★ 37–44 · 4★ 45

## Before you spend: verify

Tier lists rotate several times a year. Check current data before committing dust:

- [Pokémon GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/) — mechanics, guides, databases
- [Leek Duck](https://leekduck.com/) — events, raid bosses, egg pools
- [GamePress](https://gamepress.gg/pokemongo/) — tier lists and move data
- [Dialgadex](https://dialgadex.com/) — "best attackers *you actually own*"
- [Pokebattler](https://www.pokebattler.com/) — simulate a raid with your roster
- In-game **Search Help** — the authoritative list of storage search terms
