# Pokémon GO — A PvE Player's Guide

A practical, decision-focused guide to **Pokémon GO**, written for players who care
about **raids, Max Battles, Team GO Rocket and Gyms** — and who don't play PvP.

It is built around the questions you actually ask while playing:

> *Should I keep this or transfer it? Should I evolve it? Should I power it up?
> Should I give it a second move?*

**Last verified: September 2026.** Pokémon GO changes constantly — mechanics here are
current as of this date, but **tier lists rotate several times a year**, so verify
species recommendations against a live source before spending Stardust.

## The five rules that matter most

1. **CP is not power.** Pick attackers by species → moveset → form → level → Attack IV.
   Never by CP, and never by star rating (it's PvP-weighted).
2. **Type effectiveness beats IVs, always.** A double-super-effective hit is **2.56×**.
   A perfect Attack IV is worth about **6%**.
3. **Six at Level 40 beats three at Level 50.** Level 40 → 50 is +6.3% stats for more
   Stardust than getting to 40 cost in the first place.
4. **Don't buy second charged moves.** In a raid you only ever use your best one.
   Save it for 2–3 Team GO Rocket specialists.
5. **Never purify a good Shadow.** The 1.2× damage multiplier is worth twenty times
   the +2 IVs you'd gain.

---

## What this guide deliberately doesn't cover

**PvP / GO Battle League.** League-legal CP caps, bulk-vs-attack IV weighting, shield
baiting and move-count breakpoints all invert the advice here — in PvP, a *low*
Attack IV is often better. If you ever take up PvP, treat this guide's IV and
second-move advice as inapplicable.
