# 4. Raids & Counters

Raids are the core of PvE. This file covers how to pick counters, how to fight, and
what a good raid roster looks like.

---

## 4.1 Raid tiers

| Tier | Typical difficulty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1★** | Soloable from low level | Good candy and dust income. |
| **3★** | Soloable with a strong roster | The best solo practice. |
| **5★** | Needs 3–6+ trainers | Legendaries. The main Legendary source. |
| **Mega / Primal** | Needs 3–6+ trainers | Source of **Mega Energy**. |
| **Super Mega** | Needs **8+ trainers, all Mega Evolved** | Shield/enrage mechanic; big Mega Energy payouts. Requires **Link Charges**. |
| **Shadow Raids** | Harder; boss is enraged | Shadow Legendaries. **Remote-joinable since May 2025.** |
| **Max Battles** | Power Spots, not Gyms | Separate system — see [Max Battles](05-max-battles.md). |

## 4.2 Picking counters — the only method you need

1. **Look up the boss's type(s).**
2. **Find the double weakness first.** A 2.56× multiplier beats everything else you
   can do. (E.g. a Dragon/Flying boss takes 2.56× from Ice.)
3. **If there's no double weakness, take the highest-DPS super-effective attacker
   you own** — but check what the boss hits back with. A super-effective attacker
   that faints in four seconds contributes less than a neutral attacker that survives.
4. **Check the weather.** A weather-boosted move is another 1.2× and can change which
   counter is correct.
5. **Mega Evolve something.** +30% to matching-type attacks for the *entire lobby*,
   +10% otherwise. Always Mega Evolve before a raid.

> **The single most common mistake:** bringing six of your highest-CP Pokémon. CP is
> not damage. Use the in-game "recommended" party only as a starting point — it
> weights defence too heavily — then swap in your correct-typed attackers manually.

## 4.3 A well-rounded PvE roster

You do not need every Pokémon. You need **six good attackers for each of the six or
seven most common boss weaknesses.** Build in this order:

| Priority | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ice** | Answers Dragon, and Dragon/Flying takes 2.56×. |
| 2 | **Fighting** | Answers Normal, Steel, Dark, Rock, Ice — the widest coverage in the game. |
| 3 | **Rock** | Answers Fire, Flying, Bug, Ice; great generalist. |
| 4 | **Ghost / Dark** | Answers Psychic and Ghost bosses, which are common Legendaries. |
| 5 | **Ground** | Answers Electric, Steel, Fire, Rock, Poison. |
| 6 | **Fire / Water / Electric / Grass** | The classic triangle, fills the rest. |
| 7 | **Dragon** | Powerful neutral-ish DPS when nothing resists it. |
| 8 | **Steel / Fairy** | Niche but useful into Fairy and Dragon bosses respectively. |

### Premium tier (Legendary / Mega / Primal, as of 2026)

If you have these, they are your top slots:

- **Mega Mewtwo Y**, **Mega Rayquaza** — best-in-slot Psychic and Dragon/Flying.
- **Primal Groudon** — best Ground attacker in the game.
- **Primal Kyogre** — best Water attacker, with Origin Pulse.
- **White Kyurem** — best Ice; beats even Mega Rayquaza into double-weak Dragons.
- **Black Kyurem** — top-tier Electric/Dragon.
- **Dawn Wings Necrozma** — top Ghost, with Moongeist Beam.
- **Shadow Mewtwo** — the single best Rare Candy investment in the game.

## 4.4 How to actually fight a raid

- **Don't dodge in most raids.** Dodging costs you attack time. In a lobby with
  enough trainers, raw DPS wins. Dodge only a boss's charged move if you would
  otherwise faint mid-rotation, or in a low-headcount / solo attempt.
- **Relobbying is normal and fast.** Fainting is not failure — your six-strong team
  will be revived and re-enter. Keep Revives and Max Revives stocked.
- **Use the Ready Up button** so the lobby starts together — coordinated damage
  matters at low headcounts.
- **Party Play** (up to 4 trainers) gives a bonus attack meter and shared rewards.
  Worth forming even with one friend.
- **Watch the timer.** 5★ raids give 180 seconds plus lobby time. If the boss's HP
  bar isn't roughly halved at the halfway mark, you likely won't make it.
- **Free daily raid pass** from spinning a Gym — never let this go unused.

## 4.5 Catching the boss

- **Use a Golden Razz Berry every throw.** Catch rate matters far more than
  Excellent-throw curve bonuses for a Legendary.
- **Curveball + Excellent** is the ideal throw, but a **Nice** throw that lands beats
  an **Excellent** that misses. Prioritise consistency.
- **Raid catches have a 10/10/10 IV floor** (Level 20, or Level 25 if weather
  boosted) — the worst you can roll is 73%.
- **Mega Evolve a matching-type Pokémon before catching** for bonus Candy and
  Candy XL. At **Super Max** Mega Level this bonus is substantially larger.
